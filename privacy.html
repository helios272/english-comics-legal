Privacy Policy

English Comics Stories (the "App") · Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Who this applies to

The App is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel English Comics Daily to post the channel's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. Information the App accesses

  • When the account owner authorizes the App, TikTok provides basic profile information (such as the display name and an account identifier) and an access token that allows the App to post videos to that account.
  • The App also reads the posting options TikTok makes available for that account, and the status of each upload.

The App does not collect information about viewers, followers or other TikTok users.

3. How information is stored

Access tokens and the list of posted videos are stored only on the operator's own computer. They are not published, sold, or shared with any third party.

4. How information is used

Information is used only to upload and publish the operator's videos and to show the operator whether each upload succeeded.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the App is in use. The account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services, after which the stored token stops working and is deleted by the operator.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone other than its operator.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version. Questions can be sent through the comments or the About page of the English Comics Daily YouTube channel.
